After the [V1 release](https://www.shibaprasadb.com/2026/10/12/fundprint.html) of Fundprint, I got some feedback on what was working and what wasn't. Since then, I have been working on refining those things.

Releasing [Fundprint V2](https://lab.shibaprasadb.com/fundprint/)

Major improvement: it now runs offline! Once the website has loaded, you can switch on airplane mode or turn off your Wi-Fi, and it will still work. Absolutely fine.

The points raised by some users were valid. Even though I am not collecting any data, and someone with a software engineering background can verify that easily, there is still a trust deficit. Users need an easier way to verify the claims. That's why I added this feature.

On top of that, the UI has had a major revamp. It now looks "more mature" (at least that's what I think, let me know if you think otherwise!).

Please try it out! Looking for more feedback.
