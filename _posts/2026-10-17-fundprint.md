In my [last post](https://www.shibaprasadb.com/2026/10/12/mf-buckets.html), I did some basic exploratory analysis on my Mutual Fund statement. I started because my regular investment app wasn't giving me the deeper insights I wanted about my portfolio.

That got me thinking: how could anyone who's interested get similar insights without handing over their data to be stored somewhere?

So I built a web app called [Fundprint](https://lab.shibaprasadb.com/fundprint/).

It is currently in beta and [open source](https://github.com/shibaprasadb/fundprint). The way it works is simple: you upload your CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) and get the insights. Everything runs in your browser. Close the tab and it's all gone. Nothing is ever stored anywhere.

I have added Umami for anonymised data on visits and clicks, which I may use for funnel analysis later.

If you're reading this, please give it a try. I would love your feedback!
